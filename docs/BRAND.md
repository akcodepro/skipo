# SKIPO — Brand

**Version:** 0.2
**Status:** Exploratory

## Personality

### Should feel

- Dynamic
- Energetic
- Sportive
- Modern
- Approachable
- Playful
- Confident
- Empowering

### Should NOT feel

- Generic fitness app
- Gym-bro
- Clinical
- Corporate
- Sci-fi / futuristic
- Childish
- Overly gamified

### Overall feeling

**Grounded + energetic + optimistic + slightly unexpected.**

The app should feel like a coach that makes you want to move, not a workout tracker that gives you data.

## Colour Palette

| Colour | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Forest | `#25483E` | Foundation / structure |
| Warm Porcelain | `#F7F5EF` | Canvas / breathing room |
| Warm Orange | `#F27C45` | Action / movement |
| Golden Yellow | `#F4CF45` | Energy / achievement |
| Dusty Lilac | `#AAA3AB` | Personality / visual accent |
| Smoky Mauve | `#6B636D` | Secondary text / quiet lilac |
| Deep Brick | `#A6382B` | Error / feedback |

### Colour hierarchy

Colours should not be used equally.

**Deep Forest** builds the interface and provides visual grounding.

**Warm Porcelain** provides the main canvas and breathing room.

**Warm Orange** communicates action, movement and active states.

**Golden Yellow** communicates energy, progress and achievement.

**Dusty Lilac** adds personality and visual surprise. It should be used selectively rather than becoming another dominant accent.

### Colour principle

**Forest and Porcelain build the interface.
Orange and Yellow make it move.
Lilac makes it yours.**

The palette should feel energetic without becoming visually aggressive.

## Typography

### Display

**Chakra Petch**

Bold, distinctive and energetic.

Use for:

- Major headings
- Workout names
- Large numbers
- Timers
- Round numbers
- Primary actions
- Achievement moments

Chakra Petch is an important part of the app's personality.

### Supporting

**Bricolage Grotesque**

Readable and human, with a slightly playful character.

Use for:

- Body text
- Descriptions
- Navigation
- Labels
- Supporting information
- AI-generated workout details

### Typography principle

**Chakra Petch for impact.
Bricolage Grotesque for readability.**

Typography should contribute to the identity of the app rather than simply display information.

---

## Visual Language

The visual language should combine:

- Strong typography
- Large numbers
- Rounded UI
- Simple icons
- Playful illustrations
- Organic shapes
- Occasional hand-drawn or imperfect elements
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Purposeful colour accents

The interface can have personality without becoming decorative or childish.

## Logo & Symbol

### Core concept

The SKIPO symbol is a custom, organic **S** with a bold, dynamic silhouette.

The direction is unconventional, sculptural and energetic. The letter should feel like a distinctive shape rather than a conventional typographic character.

The symbol does not need to depict a jump rope literally. Its purpose is to establish a recognisable brand identity, not to illustrate the product.

### Visual characteristics

- Bold, organic silhouette
- Asymmetrical, flowing contours
- Dynamic and slightly unconventional proportions
- Recognisable as an S without being overly literal
- Simple enough to work at small sizes
- Distinctive enough to stand alone without the wordmark

### Usage

The symbol can be used independently as an app icon or combined with KIPO to form the SKIPO wordmark.

The wordmark uses Chakra Petch for KIPO, with the custom S designed to complement the typeface.

### Current colour treatment

- Primary: Deep Forest symbol on Warm Porcelain background.
- Alternative: Golden Yellow symbol on Deep Forest background.

The primary treatment is the current preference. Alternative colour combinations may be explored as the visual identity develops.

### Design principle

**A distinctive shape first. A recognisable S second. A literal illustration never.**

---

## Imagery & Illustration

Imagery should support movement and personality rather than rely on generic fitness photography.

Illustrations can be:

- Expressive
- Slightly playful
- Dynamic
- Simplified
- Organic

Avoid overly realistic bodybuilding or fitness imagery.

---

## Brand Principle

The app should make the user think:

**"Let's go. I can do this."**

Not:

**"Here is your workout data."**
